What Home tells you after setup, and where each setting lives.

## Home

The switch at the top shows **Home** or **System**.

Until setup is done, Home lists what is left, each linking to its screen:

- Choose a Library folder
- Add a Source
- Add a Downloader
- Add your first title

**Import what you have** adds folders of media you already own.

After setup, Home shows:

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Needs attention** or **All good** | Problems to fix, or none |
| Downloads | How many are waiting, downloading and importing |
| Sources and Downloaders | How many are working |
| **Coming up** | Air dates and releases; the owner gets a link to the Calendar |
| Shortcuts | Explore, trending, Calendar, Search |
| Uptime | How long HolyArrs has been running |

Home updates by itself. If a card says **Could not read**, press **Try again**.

## Settings

Open **Settings** from the menu. You see only the screens your account may use, and only for parts that are on. The Settings home lists what needs attention, such as a source that does not answer or no recent backup, with a link to the fix.

| Account | Sees |
|---|---|
| Owner | Every screen |
| Admin | Usual screens, plus **Import from other apps**, **Parts**, **Users and roles** and **Logs** |
| Member with **Change settings** | Usual screens |
| Everyone | **My account** |

### Common screens

| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Settings > Parts** | Turn Movies, Shows, Music, Subtitles and Requests on or off |
| **Settings > Movies and TV defaults** | Set what a newly added movie or show gets |
| **Settings > Appearance** | Theme (**Automatic**, **Dark**, **Light**), **Play trailers by themselves**, **Show Library art on the sign-in page**, and **Ratings**: up to three scores beside the year on title pages. The Library's **Score** sort always uses the members' average, whatever you pick here |
| **Settings > Library folders** | Add and check folders. See [Folders](../folders/) |
| **Settings > Backups** | Back up, and **Download key kit** |

Motion follows your device's Reduce motion setting.

## If it does not work

| You see | Fix |
|---|---|
| A Settings screen is missing | Its part is off, or your account may not use it. Ask the owner. |
| Home keeps showing setup steps | Finish all four: a Library folder, a source, a downloader and a title. |
| A folder is not found | Use the path inside the container. See [Folders](../folders/). |
