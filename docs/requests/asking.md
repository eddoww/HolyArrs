Ask for a movie, show or music that is not in the Library. The owner or an admin decides.

## Ask for a movie

1. Open **Search**, or press **Ask for something** on the Requests page.
2. Find the movie and press **Ask**.

You should see it on the Requests page as **Waiting for approval**.

## Ask for a show

1. Find the show in **Search** and press **Ask**.
2. Pick the whole show, or tick the seasons you want.

The whole show means every season except specials, plus seasons that come out later. If the show is already in the Library, you can tick only the seasons it does not keep yet.

## Ask for a 4K copy

1. In the **Ask** sheet of a movie or show, tick **4K copy**. For a title already in the Library without a 4K copy, press **Ask in 4K** on its page.
2. For a show, pick the whole show, seasons or episodes, as for a plain request.

A 4K copy is its own request, approved apart from the plain one. Approving it adds the title as its 4K copy, or adds a **4K** Version beside the copy already in the Library. Seasons are kept up to date for the whole show, so the seasons you ask for in 4K are kept for every copy of it.

## Ask for music

1. Open **Search** and switch to **Music**.
2. Find the artist and press **Ask**.
3. Pick all albums, or tick the albums you want.

Albums already in the Library cannot be ticked. For an artist already in the Library, ask for missing albums from the artist page.

## Take it back

While a request waits, open it on the Requests page and press **Take back**. You can ask again later.

## Choices

### When someone already asked

You join their request. Joining is free, does not use your weekly limit, and gets you the same news. If their request still waits and you want more seasons or albums, it grows to include yours.

### What each state means

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for approval** | Nobody has decided yet |
| **Approved** | Added to the Library |
| **Searching**, **Downloading**, **Importing** | HolyArrs is getting it |
| **Ready to watch** | Files are in. For a show: every aired episode of the asked seasons. For music: every track of the asked albums |
| **Declined** | You see the approver's reason |

You get a message on the Requests page when a request is approved, declined or ready. If the owner set up [alerts](../users/alerts.md), it can also come by Discord or email.

## If it does not work

**Symptom:** "Your account can't ask for titles yet."
**Cause:** your account lacks the right to ask. **Fix:** ask the owner.

**Symptom:** "You have used your 10 movie requests this week."
**Cause:** you hit the weekly limit for that kind. The message says how long to wait. **Fix:** wait, or ask for another kind. Movies, shows and music have separate [weekly limits](settings.md#set-weekly-limits).

**Symptom:** a season or album cannot be ticked.
**Cause:** it is already in the Library.
