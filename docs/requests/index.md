People on your server ask for movies, shows and music. The owner or an admin approves, and HolyArrs gets the title.

Open **Requests** in the menu (`http://your-server:6262/requests`).

## How a request moves

1. A member asks for a title. It shows as **Waiting for approval**.
2. The owner or an admin approves or declines it.
3. An approved title goes into the Library, and HolyArrs searches for it.
4. The request ends as **Ready to watch**, or as **Declined** with a reason.

## Who can do what

| Person | Can |
|---|---|
| Member | Ask, take back a waiting ask, see their own requests and reports |
| Owner and admins | See every request, approve or decline, answer reports, change Request rules. No weekly limit |
