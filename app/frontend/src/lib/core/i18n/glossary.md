# HolyArrs UI glossary (translation terms)

Purpose: one rendering per term, so the app UI uses the same words as holyarrs.com and docs.holyarrs.com.

Sources read (all under `site/repo`): the per-language word lists `docs/translations/<lang>/_words.md`, the docs glossary page `docs/src/content/docs/<lang>/help/glossary.md`, the docs sidebar `docs/translations/<lang>/_ui.json`, and the site strings and page titles `public/<lang>/words.js` and `public/<lang>/*/index.html`.

How to read it:
- A plain cell comes straight from the site or docs.
- A cell ending in `*` is a suggestion: the site has no rendering, so it is the plural of a sourced singular. Check it before use.
- `◆` after an English term means HolyArrs coined or reshaped the word (see `work/glossary.md`; the old-app name sits under its _Avoid_ list). Ordinary media words (movie, episode, season, album) are not marked.
- A cell with two forms (`a / b`) gives verb / noun as the word list does.
- The docs keep app labels in English and put the local word in brackets on first use. The app UI is the opposite case, so the local words below are what the UI should show.

## Notes on the table

- Some languages share one word for two places. French uses "Nouveautés" for both the News page and the "New & Popular" row; Spanish "Novedades" and Portuguese "Novos/Novidades" are close. Keep the "and popular" half when naming the Explore row.
- German: the site menu says "Hinweise" for Alerts, the docs glossary says "Benachrichtigung" for one alert. Pick one per screen.
- Settings: Spanish is always "Ajustes", Brazilian Portuguese always plural "Configurações". "Setup" is "Configuración" (es) and "Einrichtung" (de), a different word.
- Request: fr forbids "requête" (a network request). The page is Requests and one item is a Request; the two render the same in ja, zh-cn, ko, hi.
- Shows: the column uses the site menu word for "TV shows". The docs glossary uses a singular for one "Show" (serie, série, Serie, сериал, 剧集, 番組, 시리즈, शो, مسلسل).
- Words that stay in English in every language per the docs: Docker, Compose, PUID, PGID, UMASK, API, VPN, HTTPS, URL, codec, HD, 4K, product names (Plex, Jellyfin, Sonarr and so on). Also: fr keeps "release" and "seeders"; pt-br keeps "backup"; de keeps Download, Import, Update, Release, Downloader, Track as loanwords.
- Left out because the app UI does not show them: Hardlink, UMASK, PUID and PGID, URL base, Reverse proxy, Mount (setup words, see the docs glossary last section).
- Not found on the site or in the docs, so not in the table and not guessed: "Popular" alone, "Users" as a page name, "Logout".

Address, tone and number style the site and docs use. Both agree in every language.

### es (Spanish)
- Informal singular "tú". Imperatives: abre, elige, escribe, guarda. Never "usted", "vosotros" or voseo.
- Neutral Spanish, no regional words: equipo, archivo, "haz clic en", añadir, "iniciar sesión". No "por favor", "simplemente" or exclamation marks.
- Short, active sentences. Inverted marks always (¿…?). HolyArrs takes no article ("HolyArrs busca…").
- Numbers as digits with the unit: "1 minuto", "6 horas". Sizes like `2.5 GiB` stay as in English.
- Site examples: "Vota en r/HolyArrs.", "Invita a toda la familia", "Tu voto no se guardó. Inténtalo de nuevo."

### pt-br (Brazilian Portuguese)
- Informal singular "você". Imperatives: abra, escolha, digite, salve. Never "tu", "vós" or European forms (ficheiro, ecrã, utilizador).
- Brazilian words: arquivo, tela, usuário, senha, baixar, salvar, celular. "Click" is "clique em". Gerund the Brazilian way ("está buscando").
- Possessives "seu/sua". No "por favor", "simplesmente" or exclamation marks. Active voice. Spelling: ideia, e-mail.
- HolyArrs is masculine and takes the article ("o HolyArrs busca…", "no HolyArrs").
- Numbers: "1 minuto", "6 horas". Site examples: "Entre no Reddit para votar.", "Convide a família toda".

### fr (French)
- "vous", imperative for steps (Ouvrez, Saisissez, Appuyez sur). Not "tu"; infinitive only in titles and headings.
- Short sentences. No "veuillez", "il vous suffit de", "n'hésitez pas à". Plain French from France; "e-mail", never "courriel".
- HolyArrs takes no article, masculine when agreement is needed. "Appuyez sur", not "cliquez", because it also works on a phone. "saisir", not "taper".
- Typography: non-breaking space before `: ; ? !` and inside « guillemets ».
- Site examples: "Votez pour celles que vous voulez.", "Invitez toute la famille".

### de (German)
- Informal "du", lower case (dein, dir). Never "Sie", never "ihr". Imperative for steps: öffne, wähle, klicke auf. Headings in infinitive or noun form (Installieren).
- Short sentences, active voice, verb early. No "bitte", "einfach" or exclamation marks. No gender marks (no Nutzer:innen); roles are Besitzer, Admin, Mitglied.
- "anmelden / Anmeldung", not "einloggen". Backup, Update, Download and Release are used as loanwords.
- Compounds with an English or proper-name part take a hyphen (Docker-Image, API-Schlüssel). Quotes „so“. "1 Minute", "6 Stunden". HolyArrs takes no article.
- Site examples: "Melde dich bei Reddit an, um abzustimmen.", "Lade die ganze Familie ein".

### ru (Russian)
- "вы" with a lower-case в. Perfective imperatives for steps: откройте, выберите, сохраните. Descriptions in the present.
- Short sentences, no "пожалуйста", "просто", "данный", no officialese. Active verbs.
- HolyArrs is masculine and never declined. Always write «ё». Quotes «ёлочки», long dash with spaces. Sentence-case headings.
- Numbers agree with the noun (1 минута, 2 минуты, 5 минут). Library is "Медиатека", never "библиотека".
- Site examples: "Войдите в Reddit, чтобы голосовать.", "Позовите всю семью".

### zh-cn (Simplified Chinese)
- "你", never "您". Steps are short imperatives without a pronoun (打开, 点击). Plain written Mandarin, no marketing tone, no "我们" for the app, no 哦/啦/呢.
- Mainland terms: 文件夹, 服务器, 默认, 设置. Half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters or digits; full-width punctuation (，。：；？（）“”), list separator 、.
- Arabic numerals with measure words (一个来源, 一部电影, 一季, 一集, 一条请求). No plural marking.
- Site examples: "也能请求音乐", "邀请全家人", "已为 1 个想法投票。点 ▲ 撤销。"

### ja (Japanese)
- Polite です・ます, no pronoun for the reader (no あなた). Steps end in 〜します; one-off instructions 〜してください. Not plain だ/である, not keigo.
- Katakana long vowel for -er/-or/-ar (サーバー, フォルダー, ダウンローダー, オーナー, メンバー) but not -y (ライブラリ, メモリ). Half-width space between Japanese and Latin/digits; 「」 for quotes; digits, not kanji numbers.
- Counters: 1 つ, 2 回, 1 件. No plural marking. No exclamation marks or emoji.
- Site examples: "投票するには Reddit にサインインしてください。", "家族みんなを招待". Sign in is サインイン, never ログイン.

### ko (Korean)
- Statements in 합니다체 (-합니다, -입니다); instructions in -(으)세요. Never 당신 or 귀하; subject dropped. Not 해요체 for every sentence.
- Short noun-phrase headings. Loanwords follow the standard 외래어 spelling (컨테이너, 폴더, 라이브러리). Particles after English words follow how the word is read aloud.
- Arabic digits with counters (5단계, 6시간, 20개). No plural marking. No emoticons or exclamation marks.
- Site examples: "투표하려면 Reddit에 로그인하세요.", "온 가족 초대", "아이디어 1개에 투표했습니다."

### hi (Hindi)
- Polite "आप", pronoun usually dropped; steps in the -एं form (खोलें, चुनें, दबाएं). Never तुम/तू, खोलो or खोलिए. No जी, no emoji, no exclamation marks; "कृपया" only in cautions.
- Everyday Hindustani with English tech words in Devanagari (सर्वर, फ़ोल्डर, बैकअप, अपडेट): मदद not सहायता, इस्तेमाल not उपयोग. Nukta always written (फ़, ज़, ख़); sentences end with the danda (।).
- Western digits (0-9). English loan-word plurals do not change (2 फ़ोल्डर, सभी सोर्स), except फ़ाइलें and फ़िल्में. "key" is always कुंजी.
- Site examples: "पूरे परिवार को बुलाएं", "आपका वोट सेव नहीं हुआ। फिर कोशिश करें।"

### ar (Arabic)
- Second person singular masculine imperatives (افتح، اختر، اكتب). Plain Modern Standard Arabic, no dialect. Start with the verb; avoid "يتم" and "يقوم بـ".
- No "من فضلك", no exclamation marks. Right to left: Latin words and code stay left to right; do not start a heading with a Latin word. Foreign term in brackets after the Arabic (الوكيل العكسي (reverse proxy)).
- Western digits everywhere (1, 2, 6262), never ١٢٣. Arabic comma ، semicolon ؛ question mark ؟. Almost no diacritics, only where a word would be misread (مؤجَّل، مستبعَد).
- HolyArrs and other product names stay in Latin letters. Arabic plural nouns (أعضاء، نسخ احتياطية).
- Site examples: "ادعُ العائلة كلها", "لم يُحفظ صوتك. حاول مرة أخرى."
